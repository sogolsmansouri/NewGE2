It strictly validates deterministic=0/1 and sets ATen deterministic algorithms.
Torch2.1 lacks the newer deterministic uninitialized-memory-fill getter/setter,
so those calls are omitted and the compatibility difference is logged. The
unmodified source and failed first compile are preserved. Do not additionally
load libdiagnostic_deterministic.so, which forces deterministic=true regardless
of the requested timing policy.
